About

A quiet visual exploration of the Carpathians, northern forests and weather-shaped landscapes.

Michal Gažovič is a photographer based in the mountain village of Ždiar in northern Slovakia. Drawing on a background in forestry and ecology, he primarily photographs landscapes, atmospheric conditions, and the quiet spaces between. His work explores relationships between forests, people, and cultural landscapes through long-term photographic observation. His photography focuses on mountain environments, ecological processes, and the traces of transformation that shape both natural and human landscapes.
